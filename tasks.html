TRACKS & TASKS

Two tracks, five tasks

Tracks are Text and Vision. Tasks are T1–T4 within the Text Track and V1 within the Vision Track.

The examples are illustrative drafts, not released evaluation items.

TEXT TRACK

Text / LLM

Use one system for all text tasks (T1–T4).

T1 Cultural Knowledge and References

Recognition, attribution, and association of cultural references.

T2 Idioms and Conventional Expressions

Non-literal meaning, contextual interpretation, and appropriate use.

T3 Turkic Reading Comprehension

Explicit and inferential comprehension supported by a passage.

T4 Cross-Lingual Cultural Understanding

Stability of cultural knowledge and pragmatics across prompt languages.

VISION TRACK

Vision / VLM

Use one system for the vision task (V1). The same system may serve both tracks, but each track requires a separate submission and is evaluated separately.

V1 Visual Cultural Grounding

Visual recognition, attribution and association, function and cultural context.
