PARTICIPATE

How to participate

Teams may enter the Text / LLM track, the Vision / VLM track, or both.

  1. 01

    Choose a track or both

    Use one system for all text tasks (T1–T4) and one for the vision task (V1). The same system may serve both tracks, but each track requires a separate submission and is evaluated separately.

  2. 02

    Validate your submission

    Use the validation data and public evaluator after their release on 15 October 2026.

  3. 03

    Register and submit

    Registration, platform, and submission-format details will be announced before submissions open.

  4. 04

    Describe your system

    System-paper requirements and the paper schedule will be published with the final participation rules.

Registration is not open yet. Updates will be published on this site and in the project repository.
